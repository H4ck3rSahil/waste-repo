To you, from me,

Less than a year feels too short to describe what this has already been. But here we are, and it is already more than I expected — in the best possible way.

Thank you for the laughs, the long conversations, the random voice notes, and every moment that made me think okay yeah, this is a good thing. I did not say it enough out loud so I put it in a website. Very normal.

I do not know exactly what comes next. But I am really glad we are figuring it out together. Here is to whatever is ahead — and to the fact that we have barely even started.
